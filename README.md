One image that sums up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026:
37 dated stories from 9 regions, 13 with hand-drawn scenes, every one
first reported on that Thursday.

## What is on the page

- **Masthead** — "Thursday 26 November 2026", 37 stories · 9 regions ·
  13 drawn scenes · every one from this Thursday.
- **The day in UTC** — six moments, from the 00:29 Houthi strikes on
  Jeddah and Jazan to the morning's UK EU-deal vote.
- **The day in numbers** — 4,500 flights delayed, 76 hostages freed,
  ₽50tn war budget, 2,000 Canary arrivals, +36% garment pay, −2.5% Nasdaq.
- **3 headlines + 10 mid cards**, each with a flat vector scene and a
  red banner carrying the key number (Jeddah & Jazan hit, tactical
  nukes, Nasdaq −2.5%, governor fired, ₽50 trillion, 76 freed, +36%,
  4,500 delayed, 1st foreign astronaut, zero lead, EU deal vote,
  2,000 in a day, Ebola work halted).
- **Where it happened** — a dotted world map with numbered pins and
  per-region counts that add up to 37.
- **Also on Thursday, 14–37** — one-line briefs, each with its outlet.
- **Date check** — the near-miss stories deliberately left out
  (US tariffs announced 25 Nov, Cairo tanker fire 25 Nov, Riyadh tower
  attack 24 Nov, Nvidia reinstates Huang 23–24 Nov, Sinner surgery,
  Gukesh–Sindarov Game 2, Democrats' 258 seats, Huawei verdict,
  Sanofi $41bn, Dow 50,000), plus what had not happened yet.

## Sources and honesty

Every story carries a credibility label (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL /
REPORTED / CLAIM) and names its outlets (AP, Reuters, AFP, Xinhua).
Claims are labelled as claims; nothing from 25 November or earlier
is presented as 26 November news. All scenes are original drawings;
no photographs are used.

## How it runs

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library. It copies the
pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `$OUTPUT_PATH`, or
`/output/entry.png` by default). No network, no dependencies,
deterministic bytes on every run.
